About TAMP Education

Helping Children Discover Their Talents Beyond the Classroom

TAMP Education is an education consulting company based in Victoria Island, Lagos, committed to helping schools and parents develop children's creativity, confidence, discipline, leadership, and life skills through engaging extracurricular programs.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, creative, and well-rounded children through high-quality extracurricular education.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's leading provider of extracurricular learning experiences for schools and families.
