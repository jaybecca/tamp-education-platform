TAMP EDUCATION BLOG — INSTALLATION

Do NOT create another index.html.

Put these four files in the SAME folder as the existing TAMP Education index.html:
  blog.html
  blog.css
  blog.js
  blog-posts.js

In the EXISTING index.html, add this navigation item immediately after Programs:
  <li><a href="blog.html">Blog</a></li>

If the footer has Quick Links, also add:
  <li><a href="blog.html">Blog</a></li>

The blog uses your existing:
  images/logo.png
  images/favicon.png

Do not change your existing index.html structure except for the Blog link(s).
The blog contains 45 posts: 15 Parents, 15 Schools, 15 Adults.
It includes audience filters, category filters, keyword search, article reading modal, mobile navigation and conversion CTAs.
